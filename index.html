“How do you get the business if you don’t get the call?”

That’s the whole idea behind avAIlable, in one question. Here it is in plain language — what an AI receptionist is, what it does for your shop, and why there’s nothing to be nervous about.

1

What it is

An AI receptionist is a voice assistant that answers your business phone the way a trained front-desk person would — greeting callers in your shop’s name, asking the right questions, and taking down the details. Except it never sleeps: it answers 24/7, nights, weekends, and holidays, on the first ring, every time.

2

What it does for your shop

No more missed calls — and a caller who hits voicemail usually just dials the next shop on Google. While you’re on a roof or under a sink, it books the job on your calendar, covers the after-hours calls you used to lose, and gives every caller a real conversation instead of a beep. You get a text summary the second each call ends.

3

You stay in control

AI is not bad — it’s all in how you use it. Here, it’s used for one thing: answering your calls and booking your work, saying only what you approved in your Business Pack — your services, your prices, your rules. Anything urgent escalates straight to you, and it always tells callers it’s your AI assistant. No fake humans, ever.

How It Works

Your phone answers itself — in your shop’s name, with your shop’s rules

Every shop gets its own Business Pack: your services, your prices that can be quoted, your service area, your emergency rules. The receptionist only ever says what you approved — and it always tells callers it’s the shop’s AI assistant. No fake humans, ever.

1

It answers every call, 24/7

After hours, on weekends, on holidays, and during the day when the office phone rings out — the call gets answered in your shop’s name, in English or Spanish, the moment a caller speaks either one.

2

It books the job

It asks the questions you’d ask, checks the caller is in your service area, and books a real appointment window on your calendar — stating your diagnostic fee exactly the way you worded it, never inventing a price.

3

Emergencies jump the line

Burst pipe, no heat with a baby in the house, sparking panel — emergencies skip the booking script and escalate straight to your on-call person, with the recording and transcript attached to the alert.

4

You get the text

The second a call ends, you get a text summary: who, what, where, when it’s booked. The caller gets a confirmation text too — and if a caller ever hangs up or hits voicemail, a rescue text goes out so the job doesn’t dial the next shop on Google.
